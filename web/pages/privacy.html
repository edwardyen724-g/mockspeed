Privacy

Last changed 30 September 2026.

mockspeed draws grey mocks of websites and apps from what you say, on this site or through your own AI (Claude, ChatGPT and other apps that connect to mockspeed). This page says what it keeps, why, and who else handles it.

What mockspeed keeps

  • Your email address, when you sign in, to sign you in and tell your mocks apart from anyone else's.
  • Your mocks: what you and your AI said to them, the brief your AI sends (the names, words and numbers you talked over, so the mock can use them), the mock itself and its history of changes.
  • A record of each change: when it was made, where it was said (this site, the panel in your chat, or your AI), which models it used, how long it took and what it cost. This is how the day's changes are counted and how we see what mockspeed costs to run.
  • Before you sign in, a random cookie that holds the mock you made, and a keyed hash of your IP address to count mocks made without an account. We can't turn the hash back into the address.
  • If you pay, your Stripe customer and subscription ids and whether the subscription is active. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us.

When your AI connects, mockspeed sees only what the AI sends it with each call: the brief and the sentences about the mock. It does not see the rest of your conversation.

Who else handles it

  • Vercel runs this site.
  • Supabase stores accounts, mocks and the records of changes, sends the sign-in email (through Resend), and carries the live drawing to the tabs and panels watching a mock.
  • Anthropic (Claude models) writes each mock from your sentence and the brief.
  • TypeSafe (its Jev model) decides what each sentence means for the mock. It is sent the mock's outline and the sentence, not the brief.
  • Stripe takes payments.

They handle it only to provide their part of mockspeed. mockspeed does not sell your information, show ads or use your mocks to train models.

Cookies

Only what the site needs to work: the sign-in session, the no-account cookie above, and the cookie a watch link sets so that browser can watch your mocks. No tracking or advertising cookies.

How long, and deleting

Mocks are kept until you delete them from your projects. The records of changes are kept to count usage and costs. To delete your account and everything in it, or for a copy of what we hold, write to {{support}}.
